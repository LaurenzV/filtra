# Changelog

All notable changes to the crates of filtra are documented in this file. The crates are released
together and share one version.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-22

The first release of `filtra`, `filtra_types`, `filtra_kernels` and `filtra_core`, implementing the
filter effects of [Filter Effects Module Level 1](https://drafts.fxtf.org/filter-effects-1/) on the
CPU, with SIMD kernels for NEON, AVX2 and WebAssembly.

[0.1.0]: https://github.com/LaurenzV/filtra/releases/tag/v0.1.0
